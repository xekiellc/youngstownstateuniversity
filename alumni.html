Alumni Spotlight
Alumni of the Month
Jordan Steele
Class of 2014 · B.S. Exercise Science · YSU Football QB

Jordan Steele's road to the NFL is the kind of story that defines what it means to be a Penguin. After leading YSU to back-to-back FCS playoff appearances from 2011 to 2014, he went undrafted — and spent the next decade bouncing between practice squads, arena football leagues, a torn ACL, and a brief retirement. He never stopped throwing. In March 2026, at age 34, the Cleveland Browns signed him to a two-year contract as their third quarterback. At his first press conference, he held up a YSU jersey. "Youngstown made me," he said. "The Mahoning Valley never quit on me, so I never quit on the Mahoning Valley."

Cleveland Browns · 2026 YSU QB · 2010–2014 FCS Playoffs · 2× Exercise Science '14
Alumni Achievement
Medicine
Dr. Renee Okafor
Class of 2008 · B.S. Biology

After graduating from YSU with honors in 2008, Dr. Okafor completed her MD at Case Western Reserve and a cardiology fellowship at Johns Hopkins. In April 2026, she was named Chief of Cardiology at the Cleveland Clinic — one of the most prestigious positions in American medicine — at just 40 years old.

Chief of Cardiology · Cleveland Clinic · 2026
Business
Marcus Webb
Class of 2015 · B.S. Finance

After a decade in fintech, Webb returned to Youngstown to found BlockBridge Financial, a platform that helps small manufacturers access working capital through blockchain-based invoice factoring. The company closed a $28M Series B in April 2026 and has processed over $400M in transactions. Webb employs 40 people in downtown Youngstown.

$28M Series B · BlockBridge Financial · 2026
Law
Hon. Priscilla Torres
Class of 2016 · B.A. Political Science

Torres graduated from YSU in 2016 and finished first in her class at Ohio State's Moritz College of Law. After four years as a federal prosecutor in the Northern District of Ohio, she was appointed U.S. Federal Magistrate Judge in April 2026 — at 32, one of the youngest federal judges in Ohio history.

Federal Magistrate Judge · Northern District of Ohio · 2026
Science
Dr. Kevin Ngozi
Class of 2012 · B.S. Chemistry

Dr. Ngozi earned his PhD at MIT and joined NASA's Jet Propulsion Laboratory in 2019. In 2026 he was named lead materials scientist on NASA's Europa Clipper mission — responsible for developing the instruments that will analyze the subsurface ocean of Jupiter's moon Europa for signs of life.

Lead Materials Scientist · NASA JPL · Europa Clipper
