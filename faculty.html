Faculty of the Month
Dr. Ahmed Khalil
Professor & Chair · Department of Political Science
Guggenheim Fellow 2026

Dr. Ahmed Khalil joined YSU in 2011 after completing his doctorate at the University of Michigan and has since built one of the most cited research programs on post-industrial labor identity in the country. His 2023 book, The Valley and the Machine, was reviewed in the New York Times and assigned in graduate courses at Harvard and LSE. In April 2026, he became only the third YSU faculty member in the university's 118-year history to be named a Guggenheim Fellow.
