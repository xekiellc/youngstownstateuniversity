Materials Science · Nature Materials

YSU Lab Publishes Breakthrough in Biodegradable Battery Technology

A team led by Dr. Priya Nair at YSU's Advanced Materials Laboratory has published landmark findings in Nature Materials, demonstrating a new manganese-based electrochemical cell capable of powering small implantable medical devices while fully dissolving in saline solution within 90 days. The research could eliminate the need for battery-removal surgeries in millions of patients worldwide. Three major medical device manufacturers have initiated partnership discussions.
