Student Spotlight
Student of the Month
Dominique Walters
Senior · Williamson College of Business · Entrepreneurship

A first-generation college student from Youngstown's East Side, Dominique entered YSU on a full merit scholarship and spent four years turning a class project into a real business. Her agri-tech startup — which uses sensor data to reduce water waste on small farms — beat out entries from Wharton, Kellogg, and Stanford at the National Collegiate Entrepreneurship Competition, earning her a $150,000 seed investment and a spot in a Chicago-based accelerator program. She graduates in May.
